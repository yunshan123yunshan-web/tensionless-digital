## 3. A useful first check

Image: `upload/05-post-landing-page-check.png`

### Caption

If someone clicks your ad, can they immediately find the offer that caught their attention?

Check three things:

1. The headline carries the same promise as the ad.
2. The page explains what happens when someone enquires.
3. The enquiry button or form is easy to find and works on mobile.

Start by following that journey on your own phone. It is a useful way to spot friction before changing your campaign.
