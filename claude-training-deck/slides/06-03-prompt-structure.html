Prompt Structure Template

A well-structured prompt is a template you can reuse:

Role — "You are a [role] specializing in [area]"

Context — "We are [situation], and the goal is [goal]"

Task — "Please [specific action] with [specific inputs]"

Constraints — "Do not [X]. Must include [Y]. Max [Z] words."

Output — "Format as [type] with [structure]"

Verify — "Check that [criteria] before responding"

Example

RoleSenior product marketer specializing in B2B SaaS
ContextWe're launching a new analytics feature next quarter
TaskWrite launch copy for our homepage and email sequence
ConstraintsNo jargon, under 150 words, must include pricing mention
OutputTwo variants — headline + 3 bullet points
VerifyCheck tone is confident, not hype-y
