Client Email Draft

---

Context: I'm a [role] at [company]. Client is [name/company]. Relationship is [new/existing/warm].

Purpose: [Status update / Proposal / Follow-up / Check-in]

Key points to include:

- [Point 1]

- [Point 2]

- [Point 3]

Tone: Direct, professional, warm. No fluff. No "I hope this finds you well."

Format: Short email — max 5 sentences. Clear subject line. Call to action in the last sentence.

Style reference: Read writing-style.md before drafting.

---

Additional context: [Any specific context about the situation]

Template anatomy:

  • Context fills the gaps
  • Purpose drives structure
  • Tone prevents AI-isms
  • Format limits verbosity
  • Style reference personalizes

Always review. AI-drafted emails to clients need a human read before sending. Check tone, facts, and appropriateness.
