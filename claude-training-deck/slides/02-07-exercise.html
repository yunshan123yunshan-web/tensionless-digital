Exercise: Fresh Start

Practice the restart workflow with a real or simulated conversation:

Step 1

Pick a topic you've been discussing with an AI assistant recently. If you haven't, simulate a 10-message chat about planning a project.

Step 2

Ask the assistant to write a handoff summary — decisions made, open questions, next steps, key context.

Step 3

Start a new chat. Paste the handoff summary and continue from where you left off.

Step 4

Compare: does the fresh chat produce better results than the long-running one?

Bonus

Create a reusable template for handoff summaries that you can use in your daily work.
