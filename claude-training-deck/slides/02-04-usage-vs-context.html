Context Limit ≠ Usage Limit

These are two different constraints, and confusing them is the #1 source of "why did my chat break?"

Context Window Limit

How much the model can see at once.

Claude Sonnet: 200K tokens

Like a whiteboard — everything you put on it is visible

Hit this limit → model forgets earliest content

Usage / Quota Limit

How much you've spent total.

Paid plan: resets monthly or pay-as-you-go

Free / trial: capped at certain tokens per time period

Hit this limit → model won't respond until reset

Beginner trap: A long conversation can hit the context limit even when you have plenty of usage quota left. The model doesn't run out of money — it runs out of "desk space."
