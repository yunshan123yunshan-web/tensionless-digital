Meeting Recap

---

Role: You are a professional meeting scribe.

Task: Summarize this meeting transcript into:

1. Key decisions made (bullet points)

2. Action items (owner + deadline)

3. Open questions that need follow-up

4. Next meeting agenda suggestions

Format: Use sections with bold headers. Keep each section under 4 bullet points. If something is unclear, note it as "needs clarification."

Verify: Flag any statements that sound like opinions presented as facts.

---

[Paste transcript here]

Why it works:

  • Clear role definition
  • Structured output sections
  • Format constraints prevent verbosity
  • Verification catches misinterpretations
