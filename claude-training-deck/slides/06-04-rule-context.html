Rule 1: Give Context

Claude doesn't know your situation unless you tell it. Context is the difference between generic and specific outputs.

Without context:
"Write a project update."
→ Generic, could be anything

With context:
"Write a project update for our stakeholders about the migration project. They're most concerned about timeline risk. Be transparent about delays but show mitigation plans."

Context to include:

  • Who — Who is the audience? Who is the speaker?
  • What — What is the situation? What happened before?
  • Why — Why does this matter? What's at stake?
  • Goal — What does success look like?
