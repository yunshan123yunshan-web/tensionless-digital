The Simple Rule

"Use the lightest model that can do the job correctly."

Don't use Opus for sorting a list. Don't use Haiku for debugging a distributed systems issue. Match the model to the task.

When to use Haiku
Classification, summarization, data extraction, simple copy, high-volume automation.

When to use Sonnet
Most coding, content creation, analysis, planning, daily assistance.

When to use Opus
Architecture decisions, complex reasoning, research, high-stakes writing.

Pro tip: Default to Sonnet for daily work. Reach for Haiku when speed or volume matters. Reach for Opus when quality or depth matters more than cost.
