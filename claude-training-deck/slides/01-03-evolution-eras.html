How AI Has Evolved

Each era changed what was possible — and what was expected of the human in the loop.

ERA 1 · 2018–2020

Pattern Matching

LLMs as autocomplete — good for classification, extraction, simple generation. You had to know exactly what to ask.

ERA 2 · 2020–2023

Language Understanding

ChatGPT-level fluency. AI could hold conversation, follow instructions, and generate coherent content at scale.

ERA 3 · 2023–2024

Reasoning & Delegation

Models can reason through problems, use tools, follow complex instructions, and handle multi-step tasks.

ERA 4 · 2024–2025+

Agentic Workflows

AI agents that plan, execute, verify, and iterate. The human role shifts from operator to director.
