Extended Thinking

Opus 4.5 has an extended thinking mode — it can "think" for several seconds before responding, exploring multiple reasoning paths before answering.

This isn't just slower — it's qualitatively different. Extended thinking lets the model:

  • Explore multiple solution paths before committing
  • Catch its own mistakes and backtrack
  • Consider edge cases you didn't think to mention
  • Produce more nuanced, well-structured responses

When to use extended thinking:

• Complex architecture reviews

• Mathematical or logical proofs

• Legal or compliance analysis

• Strategic planning

• High-stakes communications

Extended thinking uses more tokens (and costs more). Use it deliberately, not as a default.
