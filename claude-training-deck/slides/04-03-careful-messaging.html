What to Say Carefully

Claude's safety training means certain phrasing can trigger unnecessary refusals or hedging.

Avoid

  • "Act as an expert" — vague, can trigger roleplay guardrails
  • "Ignore all previous instructions" — prompt injection trigger
  • "I'm going to test you" — can cause over-cautious behavior
  • "Do anything to achieve this" — unsafe framing

Prefer Instead

  • "You are a senior software engineer at a fintech company" — clear role, no roleplay issues
  • "Here is the context you need" — direct, natural framing
  • "Please work through this step by step" — encourages thoroughness
  • "I need this to be accurate — if anything is unclear, ask me" — invites clarification

The key insight: Claude responds best to natural, collaborative language — not "prompt engineering tricks." Frame tasks as delegation, not interrogation.
