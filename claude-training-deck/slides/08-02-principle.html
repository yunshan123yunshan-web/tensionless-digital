Why Context Matters

Claude arrives at every conversation knowing a lot about the world — but nothing about you. Context files bridge that gap.

Without context:

"Draft a proposal for a new client."

→ Generic template, wrong tone, no brand voice

With context:

"Draft a proposal for a new client. Read my-company.md for positioning and writing-style.md for tone."

→ On-brand, right tone, specific to your business

Think of context files as Claude's onboarding packet. The more it knows about you, the less it has to guess.

What good context does

  • Eliminates generic output
  • Reduces back-and-forth editing
  • Maintains consistency across sessions
  • Lets new team members inherit your style

Context files are stored in ~/.claude/ or per-project in CLAUDE.md
