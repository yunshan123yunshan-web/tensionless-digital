Modern Delegation

The mental model shift from "query" to "delegation" is the single most important skill you can learn.

Instead of: "Write a blog post about AI"

Try: "I'm writing a blog post for CTOs who are skeptical about AI. The tone should be direct and evidence-heavy. Here's the outline I have so far…"

01

Give context

Who, what, why — set the stage.

02

Set constraints

Tone, format, audience, dealbreakers.

03

Define success

What does "good" look like? Give examples.

04

Iterate

Review, refine, repeat — like a human teammate.
