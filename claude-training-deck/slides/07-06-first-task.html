Your First Cowork Task

Once your project is set up, a Cowork session follows a simple rhythm.

01

Open the project

Launch Claude in your project directory. It reads CLAUDE.md automatically.

→

02

Describe the task

Tell Claude what you need. Be specific — just like a good prompt.

→

03

Review & iterate

Claude reads files, makes changes, explains reasoning. You approve or refine.

→

04

Commit & continue

Satisfied with the changes? Commit and move to the next task.

Pro tip: Start with a small, well-scoped task. "Add error handling to this function" is better than "Refactor the entire auth system." Build trust incrementally.

After each task, review Claude's changes before accepting. Over time, you'll develop a rhythm and know when to trust the output.
