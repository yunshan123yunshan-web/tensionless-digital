Exercise: Spot the Difference

Take a task you do regularly and write two versions of how you'd ask AI to help:

Part 1 — The Old Way (2 min)

Write a single-sentence prompt with no context. Use the pattern you normally use.

Part 2 — Delegation (5 min)

Rewrite it with: role, context, specific task, constraints, and output format.

Compare the two. The second version should need less editing and produce better results — even before you click "send."
