Rule 2: Give Examples

One good example is worth 100 words of instructions. Examples show Claude what "good" looks like in your specific context.

Before:
"Write in a professional tone."
→ Vague, subjective

After:
"Write in the same tone as this example:
[paste a paragraph you love]"
→ Clear, specific, imitatable

Where examples help most:

  • Tone & voice
    Show the style you want
  • Output format
    Show the structure you expect
  • Quality bar
    Show what "good enough" looks like

Pro tip: "Few-shot prompting" — give 2-3 input → output pairs. Claude will infer the pattern and follow it.
