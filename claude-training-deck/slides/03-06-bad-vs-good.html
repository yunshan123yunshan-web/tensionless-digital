Bad Prompt to Good Prompt

Bad Prompt

"Write a blog post about AI."

No audience specified
No tone or voice
No structure or format
No examples
No constraints

Good Prompt

Role: You are a technology writer for a B2B audience.

Context: Our readers are operations leaders skeptical about AI's practical value.

Task: Write 500 words explaining 3 concrete ways AI saves time in daily operations.

Tone: Direct, evidence-backed, no hype. Short paragraphs.

Format: Headline + 3 sections with bullet points. End with a 1-sentence takeaway.

Verify: Check that every claim could be backed by a real example. Flag any overgeneralizations.

Role • Context • Task • Tone • Format • Verification

The good prompt takes 30 seconds longer to write. It saves 30 minutes of back-and-forth editing.
