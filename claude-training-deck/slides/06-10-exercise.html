Exercise: Rewrite the Prompt

Original (weak)

"Review this document and tell me if it's good."

Rewrite it using all 5 rules:

① Context
Who wrote it? Who's the audience?
② Examples
What does "good" look like?
③ Specifics
Which aspects to evaluate?
④ Format
How should feedback be structured?
⑤ Verify
What to double-check?

Bonus: After writing your prompt, try it with Claude. Does the output match what you expected? Refine and retry.
