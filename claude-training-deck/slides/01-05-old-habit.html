The Old AI Habit

Most people use AI the same way they use Google:

"Write a blog post about AI."

"What's the best way to do X?"

"Fix this email."

"Make this better."

The result? Generic outputs that need heavy editing. The AI has no context, no constraints, no understanding of what "good" looks like for you.

Problem: This approach treats AI as a vending machine — put in a coin, get a generic snack. It works, but it never delivers anything great.
