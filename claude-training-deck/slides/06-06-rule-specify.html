Rule 3: Say What You Want

Be explicit. Don't make Claude guess your preferences. Spell out your constraints.

Vague → Specific

✕ "Make it shorter"

✓ "Cut to under 200 words. Remove the third paragraph entirely. Merge the first two points."

✕ "Add some data"

✓ "Include 3 statistics from the attached report to support each claim. Cite the source."

Constraints to specify:

  • • Word / length limits
  • • Tone (formal, casual, urgent, empathetic)
  • • What to exclude
  • • Level of detail
  • • Audience knowledge level
  • • Dealbreakers / must-haves

Remember: Claude will try to give you what you ask for. If you're not specific about what you want, you'll get what you asked for, not what you wanted.
