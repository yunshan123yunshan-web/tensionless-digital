## Three introductory post drafts

These are copy drafts for organic posts on either platform. The imagery-led v2 artwork is in social-kit-v2/upload, using the existing brand's dark and steel palette, real logo and generated chrome campaign imagery. The copy addresses businesses across industries.

### 1. Introduction — pin this first

Artwork headline: Your ads. Your website. One direction.

Caption:

Meet Tensionless Digital.

We bring landing pages, Google Ads, Facebook Ads and SEO into one connected customer journey.

That means looking at the ad people click, the page they land on and the action you want them to take.

Tell us about your business and what you want your marketing to achieve. Send us a message to start the conversation.

### 2. What we do

Artwork headline: From attention to action.

Caption:

A campaign needs more than an ad.

It needs an offer people understand, a landing page that carries the message through and a clear next step.

Our services cover landing page design and development, Google Ads, Facebook Ads, SEO, lead generation and ongoing campaign optimisation.

Message us with your website and the part of your marketing you want to improve.

### 3. A useful first check

Artwork headline: Before you boost, check the page.

Caption:

If someone clicks your ad, can they immediately find the offer that caught their attention?

Check three things:

1. The headline carries the same promise as the ad.
2. The page explains what happens when someone enquires.
3. The enquiry button or form is easy to find and works on mobile.

Start by following that journey on your own phone. It is a useful way to spot friction before changing your campaign.
