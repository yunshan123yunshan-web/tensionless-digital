# Tensionless Digital — revised social launch kit

Created 24 November 2026. This is the imagery-led revision. Nothing has been published to either platform.

## Upload order

1. Use `upload/01-profile-ig-facebook.png` for both profile photos. Centre the real brand mark in the circular crop. The light file is an optional alternative; use one consistently.
2. Use `upload/02-facebook-cover.png` for the Facebook Page cover. Keep it centred and check the actual desktop and mobile upload previews before saving. Local previews are crop simulations rather than screenshots of the platform.
3. Publish `upload/03-post-introduction.png` on both pages with caption 1 from `POST-CAPTIONS.md`. Pin it if that option is available.
4. Follow with `upload/04-post-services.png`, then `upload/05-post-landing-page-check.png`, with their matching captions. Keep the full 4:5 image when posting.

These are three independent posts; they do not need to form one continuous profile-grid picture. The cover is for Facebook, while the profile images and post graphics work on both pages.

## Included files

- Two profile options: 1080×1080.
- Facebook cover: 1640×720.
- Three feed post graphics: 1080×1350.
- Post captions and suggested alt text.
- Overview and simulated cover crops in `previews/`.
- Editable artwork, bundled fonts, supplied logo and generated backgrounds in `source/`.
- Exact generation prompts, model/provider record and brand provenance in `source/`.

## How this revision was made

The backgrounds are conceptual CGI generated through the owner's authorised chr1 relay with the imagegen skill CLI, requesting GPT Image 2 at high quality. Four backgrounds were generated; the model/provider record also records the actual returned dimensions. Logo geometry and typography are composited locally so the brand and text remain accurate. These are campaign concepts, not photos of actual client work or evidence of campaign results.

The original version is preserved separately as `Tensionless-Digital-Social-Starter.zip`. The project-level `SOCIAL-PROFILES.md` remains the canonical copy record.

For editable designs, open `source/artboards.html` in a browser. Query options are `?asset=profile-dark`, `?asset=profile-light`, `?asset=facebook-cover`, `?asset=post-intro`, `?asset=post-services` and `?asset=post-checklist`. Fonts and imagery are included locally.
